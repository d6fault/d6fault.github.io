Who I Am

Rafael Eusébio, known online as D6fault, is a 13-year-old Brazilian cybersecurity prodigy whose determination and drive are propelling him to the forefront of the cybersecurity field. Starting his journey at just 11, Rafael has already earned a spot among the Top 1000 globally on TryHackMe and is ranked in the Top 90 in Brazil.

Recognized as a cybersecurity guru, Rafael has earned praise from some of the biggest names in the industry, including Srhoe, a well-known figure with over 700k followers and a massive cybersecurity Discord server Owlsec. Srhoe and others have publicly acknowledged Rafael’s extraordinary skills, reinforcing his status as a rising star in the cybersecurity community.

As D6fault, Rafael’s growing influence extends beyond his technical expertise in ethical hacking and vulnerability detection. His high level of determination, combined with his extensive network of industry professionals, fellow hackers, and aspiring cybersecurity enthusiasts, has allowed him to collaborate on high-level projects and share his knowledge with a wider audience. His network is a powerful asset that not only helps him grow but also enables him to mentor and inspire others in the community.

Rafael’s passion for cybersecurity, fueled by his unrelenting desire to understand and protect digital systems, is matched by his commitment to educating others. Through his tutorials, live hacking sessions, and security tips, he’s building a community of like-minded individuals who are all eager to learn and grow in the field.

With his extensive network, relentless determination, and ever-expanding influence, Rafael is poised to become a leading voice in the cybersecurity industry, shaping the future of digital security and inspiring the next generation of cyber defenders.
