About

            

                

                    
D6fault is an American security researcher known for identifying and responsibly disclosing cybersecurity vulnerabilities in government and corporate systems.

                    
He began his practical training on TryHackMe at age 13, completing 300+ rooms and developing hands-on expertise across penetration testing and web application security. By 14, he had reported security issues affecting U.S. government entities — including the Department of Defense, Department of Education, Department of Health and Human Services, the Department of Energy, and the Department of Justice.

                    
His work focuses on web security, ICS/OT security, firmware reverse engineering, vulnerability discovery, and ethical disclosure through responsible reporting.
