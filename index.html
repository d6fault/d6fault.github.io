About

            

                

                    
D6fault is an American security researcher known for identifying and responsibly disclosing cybersecurity vulnerabilities in government and corporate systems.

                    
He began his practical training on TryHackMe at age 13, completing 300+ rooms and developing hands-on expertise across penetration testing and web application security. By 14, he had reported security issues affecting U.S. government entities — including the Department of Defense, Department of Education, and the Department of Health and Human Services.

                    
His work focuses on ICS/OT security, firmware reverse engineering, vulnerability discovery, ethical disclosure, and improving system security through responsible reporting and educational content creation.

                    CVE-2026-23695
                    severity: medium
                    

                        Cockpit CMS <= 2.14.0
                        
Stored XSSauthenticatedVue v-html

                        
Stored cross-site scripting in the "Set" field type's Display template option. User-supplied input is processed by an unsafe function and rendered with Vue's v-html directive without sanitization, allowing an authenticated attacker with specific permissions to inject JavaScript into other users' browsers.
